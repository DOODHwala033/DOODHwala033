## What the top of the list has in common
- **Title formula:** "How [famous power] Actually Works" or "The Real Science of [power]". The promise is a hidden truth about a power fans already know.
- **Fandom + real world:** the power name is the hook for anime fans, and "in real life / by science" opens it to people who don't watch the show.
- **Top 10 are mostly "Actually Works":** Haki, Kaioken, Kamehameha, Ultra Instinct, Super Saiyan, Gear 5.
- **Black Flash (1.1M)** fits the same formula and is the one we decoded.
- **Weaker titles (under 250K):** the Sukuna domain, Zenitsu, One For All, Gojo Infinity (second version). Some of these cover powers that are already popular, so the difference is probably the title hook and thumbnail rather than the topic alone.
