# Wheel Battle Ep2 · Script v4 (built on the "How ... Actually Works" pattern)

Status: draft for approval. Not yet recorded or timed.

## What changed from v3
- **Title and hook follow the top-performing formula.** Each top Short names a famous power and promises a mechanic ("How ... Actually Works"). v4 opens with a question that promises the same: how a Spirit Ball beats the one monster that adapts.
- **Explain as steps.** v3 was a story. v4 gives the viewer the rule first (3 steps), then shows it on screen, like the Black Flash Short.
- **A rule the viewer takes away:** "Mahoraga adapts once. The same move stops working."
- **Comment question at the end**, tied to the rule.

## Narration (one line per shot)
1. Mahoraga adapts to any attack. Once.
2. So how did Yamcha's Spirit Ball hit it? Here's how it actually works.
3. Step one: a Spirit Ball is steered with two fingers, around the guard, straight into the wheel.
4. Step two: the wheel turns, and it adapts.
5. Step three: the same attack fizzles. Zero effect.
6. You asked Yamcha for an autograph, and called Wolf Fang Fist cool.
7. Nobody has ever said that to him. So now... you're family.
8. Then the ground shakes. Yamcha looks up... and up.
9. This fight has a height requirement.
10. Wolf Fang Fist! Everything he has, all at once.
11. And Mahoraga feels... a massage.
12. One hit. Crater. Face-down.
13. Saibaman flashbacks. He calls Vegeta.
14. I watched you lose to a plant. No.
15. But Yamcha gets back up.
16. Two fingers. One Spirit Ball. Around the guard... straight into the wheel.
17. Direct hit!
18. Then the wheel turns. Click. It adapted.
19. He fires again... and it fizzles.
20. The wheel turns again. This time, it's looking at you.
21. Your heartbeat. Your breathing.
22. You're teaching it how to find you.
23. Footsteps. Sukuna.
24. Shibuya taught me how to kill you.
25. Mahoraga roars... and another shadow steps in.
26. Now you know the rule. Who should the wheel pick next? Comment it.

## Hook (0–3 s)
- Frame 0: the title card "HOW YAMCHA ACTUALLY BEAT MAHORAGA" (the title formula on screen).
- Frame 1–2: the wheel, Yamcha, and the question line.
- The first caption is in the top third so the title and the voice do not compete.

## Note on claims
- This is a fan crossover interpretation. The "rule" (adaptation after one exposure) is an editorial explanation, not a canon ability. Label it "creative crossover interpretation" in the description.

# Part 2 · "How Sukuna Actually Beats Mahoraga"

Length: about 60 s. Picks up from "another shadow steps in".

## Title and hook
- Title: **"How Sukuna Actually Beats an Unbeatable Monster"** (follows the pattern).
- 0–3 s: "Mahoraga adapts to anything. So how do you beat it?" Then a cut to the shadow.

## Rule the viewer learns
"Mahoraga adapts to a move after one exposure. To win, you must change the rule, not repeat the move."
