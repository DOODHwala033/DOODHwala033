# Wheel Battle Ep2 · Script v3 (hook rewrite, Part 2 plan below)

Status: draft for approval. Not yet recorded or timed. Re-run voice, captions and cut timing after approval.

## Why v3 changed (from the Black Flash decode)
- **Loss first.** The Short opens on a small loss ("it almost flew off the chain") in 3 seconds. v3 opens on the scam verdict and the threat in the first line.
- **Second person throughout.** Every line talks to the viewer ("you asked", "you're family", "you're teaching it").
- **Escalation.** Each proof is bigger than the last: autograph → height requirement → massage → Spirit Ball hit → fizzle → heartbeat.
- **Turn.** The wheel stops pretending to be a game at "it adapted". The tone shifts from comedy to dread.
- **Close on the viewer.** The last line puts the viewer's own pick on the wheel and asks for a comment.

### Decision needed before we write the reveal
The storyboard says the fourth shadow is never shown. Part 2 needs one of these:
- **A. Keep it hidden** and end on the question. Strongest cliffhanger, lowest risk.
- **B. Reveal a named character** (for example a crossover you pick). That is a bigger payoff but needs a designed silhouette and voice.
- **C. Reveal Mahoraga's wheel landing on a face** and cut.

Recommendation: A for Part 2, and let the comments choose the reveal for Part 3. The format already asks viewers to comment.

### Engagement goals (hypotheses to test)
- Hook in 3 seconds with a loss, a threat and a name.
- A comment prompt in the last line, tied to the wheel mechanic.
- One unanswered question per part so people come back.
- Keep the pinned comment as a question too (see the storyboard).

## Guardrails (from the storyboard)
- Mahoraga never speaks. Sukuna's attacks have no gore; the crater is a comic beat.
- The adaptation mechanic is a fictional escalation. Do not show a canon ability that is not in the source.
- The fourth shadow stays featureless until the decision above is made.
