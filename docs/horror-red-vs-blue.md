# RED vs BLUE — the night they came for your door (Instagram Reel, ~29 s, 9:16)

**Logline.** Tonight twelve monsters fight over one house. Team RED wants in. Team BLUE guards the door. You are the one behind it. The reel ends on a vote: *Red… or blue?*

**Format.** 1080×1920, 30 fps, 28.7 s, deep Kokoro voice (`am_onyx`), synthesized horror score and SFX, word-timed captions. Built in code: three.js (toon shading, ink-line pass, depth of field) rendered frame by frame in headless Chromium, mixed and encoded with ffmpeg.

## The two teams

All twelve are original character designs. The folklore creatures come from public-domain legend; the three "movie-style" slashers are archetypes (a mask, a power tool, a trap-maker), not the studios' trademarked characters, so the reel is safe to post.

| 4 | **Chudail** | floating, hair over her face, backward-turned feet, long nails | Indian folklore |
