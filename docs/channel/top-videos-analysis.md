## What the decoded Black Flash video does to keep people watching
1. Opens on a small loss ("it almost flew off the chain") in 3 seconds.
2. Every claim is shown on screen with a diagram or a comparison.
3. Escalating proof, a turn at 47 s, and three numbered steps.
4. Closes on the viewer's own bag ("the next time you hit that bag").

## Why people watch (working hypotheses, to test)
1. **Curiosity gap in the title** (how it actually works).
2. **Value in one takeaway:** the viewer leaves with a rule they can use or repeat.
3. **Fan identity:** naming the famous power makes fans feel they're in on it.
4. **Visual proof:** each claim is a picture, not just a voice-over.
5. **Comment trigger:** an open question at the end gives people a reason to reply.
