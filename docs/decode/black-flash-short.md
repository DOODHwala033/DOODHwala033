## 3. Hook and emotion
- **Open with a loss, not a reveal.** The first 3 s show a routine bag hit (×98) and then the one shot that breaks the chain. That is a curiosity gap in 3 s.
- **Second person throughout** ("you", "your punch"). Every claim is pointed at the viewer.
- **Build by contrast.** Black belt vs gym-goer, 50% harder, 10 ms vs 28 ms. Each proof makes the next one feel bigger.
- **A turn at 47 s** ("That's your flash.") moves from evidence to instruction, so the viewer knows the payoff is coming.
- **Three numbered steps** give it a checklist feel. People stay to see if they can do the steps.
- **Close on the viewer's own bag.** The last line brings the story back to the viewer's life.
