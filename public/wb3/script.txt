Mahoraga adapts to everything.
So how do you beat it?
Rule one: it learns one move at a time.
Yamcha throws Wolf Fang Fist.
The wheel turns... and it adapts.
Now that move is useless.
Rule two: never repeat yourself.
Doctors beat bacteria the same way.
One antibiotic, and bacteria adapt.
Two at once, and they can't keep up.
So Yamcha stops using one move.
He combines two.
Rule three: attack inside the delay.
Every adapting thing has a delay.
Even your brain needs a quarter of a second to react.
The wheel needs time to turn.
Yamcha waits for the click...
Then he goes.
Wolf Fang Fist and Spirit Ball, at once.
Two attacks. One wheel.
The wheel stalls.
Mahoraga can't answer both.
Direct hit!
Yamcha's first real win.
The wheel cracks... and Mahoraga dissolves into gold light.
No, really.
But the ground is still shaking.
