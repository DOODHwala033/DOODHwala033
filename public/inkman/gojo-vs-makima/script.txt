Could Gojo defeat Makima…
without killing a single innocent person?
Gojo's Infinity makes him
almost impossible to touch.
But Makima doesn't need to hit him.
She's the Control Devil…
and she can control beings
she considers beneath her.
And here's the terrifying part.
Makima has a contract
with Japan's Prime Minister.
Any fatal damage dealt to her…
gets transferred to a random Japanese citizen
as an illness or accident.
So killing her might mean
killing someone completely innocent.
Gojo realizes brute force isn't enough.
He activates Unlimited Void…
flooding Makima's mind
with endless information.
For a moment…
it looks like the fight is over.
But Makima's contract
doesn't simply disappear
because she's incapacitated.
And if Gojo tries to finish her…
another innocent person
could pay the price.
Gojo might win the battle.
But can he defeat Makima…
without becoming the reason
someone else dies?
Who wins this fight?
